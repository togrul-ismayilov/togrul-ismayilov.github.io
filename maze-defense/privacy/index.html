Maze Defense: Privacy Policy

Effective date: 11 November 2026

Maze Defense ("the game") is a game for Android made by ISVA Games ("we", "us"). This policy explains what information the game and the services it uses handle, and the choices you have.

Summary

  • The game itself collects no personal information. Your progress and settings stay on your device.
  • There are no accounts, no purchases, and no analytics of our own.
  • The game shows an ad only when you choose to watch one, to earn a bonus in the game. These rewarded ads come from Google AdMob, which collects certain information, as described below.

Information the game keeps on your device

Your progress, Crystals, settings, and saved games are stored only in the game's private storage on your device. We never receive them.

  • Deleting them. Uninstalling the game, or clearing its storage, deletes them.
  • Backups. The game turns off Android's cloud backup for this data, though Android may copy it to a new phone during a device-to-device transfer.

Ads by Google AdMob

When the game asks for an ad or shows one, Google's Mobile Ads SDK collects and processes information such as:

  • device identifiers: your device's advertising ID and app set ID;
  • IP address, from which an approximate location may be inferred;
  • interactions with ads and the game, for example that an ad was shown or tapped;
  • diagnostic and performance information.

Google's User Messaging Platform, which shows the consent form and stores your choice, also communicates with Google.

Google uses this information to show and measure ads, for analytics, and to prevent fraud and abuse. Ads may be personalized where you consent, or where the law allows it. Google's handling of this information is described in:

  • the Google Privacy Policy;
  • How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services.

The ads are limited to content rated PG at most. We do not receive the information Google collects about you. From Google we receive only reports about ads in total, for example how many were shown.

Your choices

  • Watching ads is always optional. The whole game is playable without them. With no connection, no ads are requested.
  • Consent (European Economic Area, United Kingdom, Switzerland). On first launch the game shows Google's consent form, and no ad is requested before you answer. You may consent or decline. If you decline, Google may still show non-personalized or limited ads, and the game works the same. You can change your choice at any time in Settings → Privacy choices.
  • United States. Where Google offers an opt-out of the sale or sharing of personal information under US state privacy laws, it appears in Settings → Privacy choices.
  • Advertising ID. You can reset or delete your advertising ID in your device's settings, under Privacy or Google, then Ads.

Children

The game is meant for people aged 13 and older and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Keeping and deleting data

We hold no personal information about you, so we have nothing to delete. The game's data on your device is deleted when you uninstall the game or clear its storage. For the information Google collects, use Google's controls described above, such as resetting your advertising ID, or contact Google.

Security

Google's SDKs send their data encrypted in transit (TLS). The game's own data stays in its private storage on your device.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be published at this address with a new effective date.
